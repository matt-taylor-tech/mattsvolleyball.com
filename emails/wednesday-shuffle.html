How the format works

We call it Level Up. Every team starts each night at Level 1 with light social handicaps (things like learning teammate names and quick communication cues) designed to help a brand new lineup click fast. Win a game, your team levels up and picks up a tougher handicap for the next match. Lose, you stay put. The result: stronger teams get handicapped as they win, and games stay competitive all night.

Each season runs 7 regular nights plus a playoff night. Check in by 6:30, teams and schedules are generated around 6:40, and you'll typically get four games over about two hours.
